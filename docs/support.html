FAQ

Can I undo a rename? Yes. Open History and undo any batch, including the folders it created.

Where are my files? In the Files app → On My iPhone → Lote, or wherever you exported them.

How do I restore Lote Pro? Settings → Lote Pro → Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Lote.
